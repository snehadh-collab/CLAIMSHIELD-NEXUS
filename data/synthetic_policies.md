# CLAIMSHIELD NEXUS SYNTHETIC HEALTHCARE POLICIES

## SECTION 101: DUPLICATE BILLING & TIMING RULES
1. Multiple claims submitted for the exact same CPT procedure code, same member, and same provider within a 24-hour window without a distinct modifier flag are strictly prohibited and classified as potential duplicate billing.
2. Impossible Travel/Timing Policy: Claims submitted for the same member across two geographically distinct facility locations (>50 miles apart) within a 2-hour window are deemed physically impossible and flagged for immediate fraud investigation.

## SECTION 102: UNBUNDLING & UPCODING ANOMALIES
1. Unbundling: Billing separately for constituent laboratory/surgical components that are normally billed under a single comprehensive CPT code is prohibited under Medicare/Medicaid billing guidelines.
2. Upcoding: Consistent billing of highest-tier Evaluation & Management (E&M) codes (e.g., CPT 99215) exceeding 4x the peer group baseline average for similar specialty providers triggers mandatory SIU audit review.

## SECTION 103: PHANTOM BILLING & FACILITY FRAUD
1. Services billed by a provider for members who have no recorded admission, appointment, or prior clinical record at the specified facility are classified as Phantom Billing under FWA Penalty Rules.
2. Coordinated Ring Activity: Any network of 3 or more providers sharing tax IDs, residential addresses, or bank accounts with recurring patient-routing loops will be subjected to immediate payment hold and referral to federal authorities.
